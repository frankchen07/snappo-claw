## Recommended Treatment Plan

### Step 1 — Stage (this week)
- 3-view chest radiographs + abdominal ultrasound
- Rules out metastatic disease before investing in local treatment
- Expected result: clean (Grade 1 STS mets in ~11% of cases)

### Step 2 — Characterize the palpable lump
- Ask Arkans or Kiselow to assess at consult
- FNA if there's any question — is this scar tissue or residual/growing tumor?
- This affects radiation protocol choice

### Step 3 — Radiation Therapy (primary recommendation)
- **Why radiation:** Strongest evidence base for incompletely excised STS. Forrest et al. showed median survival of 5.1 years, 68% alive at 3 years. Speck's favorable Grade 1 biology should perform at or above these numbers.
- **Full protocol:** ~19-20 daily treatments, 3 Gy/fraction, ~57 Gy total. Brief daily anesthesia. Side effects: skin inflammation at site, 1-3 weeks, manageable with meds + E-collar.
- **Short protocol (SRS):** 1-3 treatments, higher dose per fraction. Ask Arkans if this is appropriate for Speck's situation — may be ideal for small, well-defined microscopic residual disease. Less proven for STS specifically but emerging data is promising.
- **CT scan required** for radiation planning (part of the process).

### Step 4 — Consider Adding Metronomic Chemo Post-Radiation
- Cyclophosphamide (10-15 mg/m²/day) + piroxicam (0.3 mg/kg)
- Complementary mechanism: radiation handles local disease, metronomic handles angiogenesis + immune modulation
- Elmslie et al. showed dramatically delayed recurrence in incompletely resected STS (P<.0001)
- Low cost (~$50-100/month) + periodic bloodwork/urinalysis
- **Risk to monitor:** sterile hemorrhagic cystitis (3% severe in literature). Furosemide (Lasix) co-prescribed to reduce risk.
- Ask Kiselow if he'd recommend this as adjunct to radiation

### Step 5 — Long-Term Monitoring
- STS site: recheck every 3 months for year 1, every 6 months years 2-3
- Full-body skin checks for new MCTs every 2-3 months (any new lump → FNA immediately)
- Recurrences documented as late as 1,395 days — this is a years-long commitment
